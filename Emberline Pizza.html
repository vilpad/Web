About Emberline

One oven. Four pizzas.

We make fewer pizzas than most kitchens and we make each one the same way every time.

The oven is a wood-fired dome that runs hotter than a home oven can reach by a factor of two. At that heat the crust puffs, the edge chars in leopard spots, and the cheese melts before the base can dry out. That is why the whole bake takes about ninety seconds.

The dough ferments in the cold for three days. The longer rest gives it flavor and a light, open crumb that stays chewy instead of bready. Toppings are kept simple so you can taste the dough, the tomato and the smoke.
